Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how information may be collected, used, as well as handled when you visit or contact WearHaus.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What We Collect

When you place an order or send us a message, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. We also note the shirt, size, and quantity you choose so we can prepare your order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your details only to confirm and deliver your order, answer your questions, and tell you about any problems with your purchase. We do not use your information for anything unrelated to your order.

Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal information. We only share your address with delivery partners who need it to bring your order to you.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have rights regarding how your personal data is handled.
